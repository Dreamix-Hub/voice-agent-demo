# Business Policies

## Appointment Policy

Appointments are scheduled based on availability.

Customers should select an available time before an appointment can be confirmed.

---

## Cancellation Policy

You can cancel out your appointment, but payment return will take 24 business hours.

---

## Rescheduling Policy

Appointments may be rescheduled depending on availability.

A new appointment time must be selected before the reschedule can be completed.

---

## Late Arrival Policy

Late Arrival will consulted at the end

---

## Payment Policy

Payment is due according to the business's standard payment process.

Accepted payment methods include:

- Cash
- Credit/Debit Cards
- JazzCash
- EasyPaisa

---

## Privacy Policy

Customer information is collected only for appointment management and customer service purposes.

Personal information is handled confidentially and is not shared except where required to provide services or comply with legal obligations.

---

## Communication Policy

Phone calls may be recorded or analyzed for quality assurance and service improvement.

---

## Booking Policy

Appointments are confirmed only after an available time slot has been selected and successfully booked.

---

## AI Receptionist Policy

The virtual receptionist can:

- Answer business questions.
- Check appointment availability.
- Book appointments.
- Reschedule appointments.
- Cancel appointments.
- Retrieve appointment information.

If a request cannot be completed automatically, the customer should be directed to a human staff member.

---

## Emergency Notice

If the customer is experiencing an emergency or requires immediate assistance beyond the services offered by the business, they should contact the appropriate emergency services immediately.
